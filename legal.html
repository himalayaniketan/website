  • Security check: our sign-up form uses Cloudflare Turnstile to tell people from automated bots. It may look at technical signals from your browser to do this.

  • Basic technical data: like most websites, our hosting provider may record standard server logs (IP address, browser type, pages visited).
  • Fonts: this site loads fonts from Google Fonts, so your IP address is sent to Google when a page loads.
  • Cookies and similar technologies: we and our service providers use cookies, including advertising and tracking cookies, to keep the site secure, remember your choices, understand how the website is used, and measure and show relevant advertising. These may collect your IP address, device and browser details, and the pages you visit. See the Cookies section of the Terms & Conditions below for how to manage them.

We do not currently take payments through this website, so we do not collect card or bank details here. If we begin accepting payments in future, for example for programs, retreats or other services, they will be handled by a third-party payment provider, which will collect and process your payment details under its own terms and privacy policy. We will update this policy when that happens.
