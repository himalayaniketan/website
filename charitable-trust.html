Seva

Himalaya Niketan Charitable Trust exists for one simple reason: to serve. Guided by Mataji, the trust reaches out to those who have little, and to those who have given their lives to the spiritual path. There is no business here, only offering.

Our Seva

Free Health Camps

The trust holds free health camps for the poor and those in need. Care is offered with dignity, humility and without charge, because no one should go without help for want of means.

Free Spiritual Books

Vidya (Knowledge) should never be locked behind a price. The trust distributes spiritual books free of cost, so that every seeker, rich or poor, can find a lamp for the journey.

Service of Sadhus and Saints

Those who renounce the world to walk the Adhyatmik path are also in need of care. The trust serves sadhus and saints with respect and devotion, seeing in each of them a reflection of the Divine.

Gau Seva

The sacred cow has long been a symbol of compassion in Indian life. The trust serves cows with food and care, as an act of gratitude and reverence for all living beings.

The Source

Mataji’s Guidance

The trust’s work flows from Mataji’s guidance and the spirit of selfless giving. The seva continues even in Mataji’s physical absence, in her name and with her energy.

Seva is not what we do. It is who we are.

Donations & Volunteering

The trust is not presently accepting donations or volunteer activities from the general public. Please follow the updates on this website for any changes in future.
