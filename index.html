Surrender to the Existence

Not a Guru of the jungle, but a light for the world.

Born in the Himalayan foothills, she is Monika to her family, Dr. Monika Parsheera to her patients, Ma Someshwari Devi to Sadhus, Sants and Pirs, and Mataji to her followers and devotees. Mataji walks a path for the 21st century.

She lives within society, never above it, and guides seekers to move through life with awareness, to enjoy it, and to see it for what it is. Hers is a practical road, never extreme.

In her presence, guards drop, hearts open, and the seeker rises effortlessly, like boats lifted by a rising tide.

The Trust

Beyond the Ashram, Mataji also runs the Himalaya Niketan Charitable Trust, which exists for one reason: to serve. Through free health camps, free spiritual books, service of sadhus and saints, and gau seva, it reaches those in need. There is no business here, only offering.

Goa & Rishikesh

Himalaya Niketan Clinics

Himalaya Niketan also runs clinics in Goa and Rishikesh, offering Nadi Chikitsa and wellness consultation. The clinics are separate from the charitable trust, and consultation fees apply.

What the clinics earn goes to seva. Mataji donates the income earned at the clinics to the Himalaya Niketan Charitable Trust, which funds free health camps, free spiritual books, service of sadhus and saints, and gau seva.

Please note

Mataji’s availability at the clinics is seasonal and spontaneous. At her own discretion, she may attend to someone in need, but this cannot be requested or assured.
